Privacy Policy

How your booking information is used, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Who operates Allthatbook

Allthatbook is operated by Cadmus Chau, an individual based in Singapore. For privacy questions, access or correction requests, withdrawal of consent, or deletion requests, email itscadmus@gmail.com.

2. Information you provide

We process account identifiers and email addresses used to sign in, and basic profile information supplied by Google if you choose Google sign-in; booking details you enter or save, including accommodation, dates, prices, payment status, cancellation terms, references and notes; and PDFs, screenshots, pasted text or booking-page text you submit for import. These materials may contain traveller names and other personal information. Only submit information you are entitled to share, and remove passport numbers, full payment-card details and other unnecessary sensitive information before importing.

3. Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate you, store and display your bookings, extract details for your review, organise deadlines, create calendar reminders at your request, respond to support requests and maintain service security. Imports can be inaccurate: review the extracted details before saving them.

4. AI-assisted imports

When you choose to read an import, its text, PDF or image content is sent through our backend to Anthropic for AI-assisted extraction. This may include personal information contained in your submission. The resulting booking fields are returned for review; saved booking records are stored separately. Manual entry is available if you do not want to submit documents for AI processing. Provider processing and retention are subject to the applicable service terms; do not assume uploaded content is immediately erased from all provider systems.

5. Browser extension

When you ask the extension to capture a booking page, it reads selected text or visible page content and related page information to prepare an import. You can combine pages for one reservation and review the captured information. Capture drafts are held in extension session storage and transferred to the Allthatbook tab’s session storage when you request review. Drafts have a 30-minute validity check; expired stored drafts are removed when the relevant checks run, rather than by a guaranteed deletion timer. You can discard captures in the extension and cancel the pending import in the app. The extension checks supported booking-page domains and finds an existing Allthatbook tab to reuse; it does not collect general browsing history. Reading the import submits the captured content for the AI processing described above. The extension is not intended to collect your general browsing history or your booking-platform password.

6. Google Calendar access

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. With your authorisation, Allthatbook uses permission to manage events on calendars you own to create deadline events in your primary calendar and retrieve or update matching events previously created through this integration. Event data sent to Google includes the booking name, destination, booking platform, cancellation or payment deadline, reminder settings and a stable identifier derived from the booking ID. It does not include your booking notes, price or reservation confirmation code. When updating a matching event, the app retrieves that event from Google to check its status and identifying metadata. Although the permission allows broader event management, this integration is intended for your Allthatbook deadline events, not general calendar analysis. Google Calendar access tokens are used in browser memory to send requests directly to Google. This calendar integration does not save those tokens in browser storage or send them to our booking database. This is separate from the sign-in session described below. Calendar event identifiers and matching metadata are used to avoid creating duplicate events. Events remain in your Google account under Google’s settings.

7. Limits on Google data use

Allthatbook’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or credit decisions, or use it to train general-purpose AI models. Google Calendar API data is not sent to our AI booking-import service. We restrict human access to Google user data to circumstances permitted by that policy, such as your explicit agreement for support, security investigations, or legal obligations.

8. Service providers and sharing

We use Supabase for authentication, database and backend processing, Vercel for website hosting, Anthropic for requested AI imports, and Google for the optional calendar integration. These providers receive the information necessary for their services and may process technical information such as IP addresses, request details and diagnostic logs. The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and loads Google’s identity script when opening the configured calendar connection. Google receives the technical request information needed to deliver those resources. Support messages are handled through our contact email provider. We may disclose information when legally required or when reasonably necessary to protect users and the service. We do not sell your personal information.

9. Browser storage, security and international processing

Guest bookings are stored in this browser’s local storage until removed or browser data is cleared. Signed-in bookings are stored in Supabase under your account. The app persists its Supabase sign-in session in browser storage so you can remain signed in; this is distinct from the temporary Google Calendar access token. Extension imports use session storage. The app does not include advertising trackers or an analytics SDK. Clearing browser storage can remove locally held data and sign you out. We use access controls and secure connections to protect data, but no system is completely secure. Our service providers may process data outside your country, including outside Singapore. Where applicable, we are responsible for arranging safeguards required by data-protection law for these transfers.

10. Retention and deletion

Saved account and booking information is kept while needed to provide the service and for legitimate security or legal purposes. You can remove saved bookings through the app. There is currently no self-service account-deletion button. Email itscadmus@gmail.com to request account deletion, access, correction or withdrawal of consent; we may need to verify that the request is yours. We delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable legal requirements. Copies in backups and operational logs may remain until their retention cycles expire. The import endpoint does not deliberately save raw uploaded files to a document store, but it logs request failures and a limited portion of provider error responses; those diagnostics may contain information included in the provider’s error. Hosting and AI providers may also retain processing records under their service terms. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup or provider system. Local browser copies, exported files and events in your calendar are separate: deleting an Allthatbook booking or account does not automatically remove them.

11. Your calendar choices

You can revoke Allthatbook’s Google access through your Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents further authorised access but does not delete events already created. Remove unwanted events in your calendar. Downloaded calendar files are controlled by you and whichever calendar app you import them into.

12. Children and updates

Allthatbook is not intended for children under 18. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted personal information. We may update this policy as the service changes and will show the revised date here. We will provide additional notice or obtain consent when required for a material change in data use.
